# E-commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard

An interactive **E-commerce Sales & Performance Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel** to analyze sales, customer behavior, product performance, shipping time, and customer satisfaction.

## Project Overview

This dashboard provides a consolidated view of e-commerce performance and allows users to explore key business metrics using interactive slicers and visualizations.

The dashboard focuses on answering questions such as:

- How are sales and quantity trending over the last 13 weeks?
- How do customers prefer to purchase?
- Which products are most popular?
- Where are customers located?
- How many products do customers typically buy?
- How long does it take to ship orders?
- How satisfied are customers?

## Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks the following high-level metrics:

- **Quantity:** 11,997
- **Amount:** $649.0K
- **Orders:** 2,400
- **Average Rating:** 4.0
- **Average Days to Deliver:** 2.3 days

## Dashboard Features

### 1. 13-Week Sales Trend
Visualizes the weekly trend of:

- Sales Amount
- Quantity Sold

This helps identify changes and patterns in sales performance over time.

### 2. Customer Purchase Preferences
Analyzes how customers purchase across different channels:

- App
- Instagram
- Partner App
- Target.com
- Website

The data is further segmented by gender.

### 3. Popular Products
Displays product performance by gender and highlights popular product categories such as:

- T-Shirts
- Sneakers
- Bikinis
- Sundresses
- Hoodies & Sweatshirts
- Crop Tops
- Casual Dresses
- Pajama Sets
- Sunglasses
- Baseball Caps

### 4. Customer Location
A geographic visualization shows the distribution of customers and sales across the United States.

### 5. Purchase Quantity
Customers are grouped according to the number of products purchased:

- 1–5
- 6–10
- 11–16
- Above 16

### 6. Shipping Performance
Analyzes the number of orders according to shipping duration, helping identify delivery patterns.

### 7. Customer Satisfaction
Customer ratings from **1 to 5** are visualized across different months to understand satisfaction trends.

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters
